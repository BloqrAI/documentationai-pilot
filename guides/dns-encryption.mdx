# DNS Encryption & Privacy Guide

Learn how DNS encryption protects your privacy, how Bloqr works with encrypted DNS resolvers, and how to set up encrypted DNS across your devices.

## Why DNS Encryption Matters

Your internet service provider (ISP) can see **every domain you visit**, even if the websites themselves are encrypted with HTTPS.

<Callout kind="tip">
  DNS encryption protects your query privacy from your ISP.
</Callout>

## Next Steps

1. Choose your resolver - Start with Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 for simplicity
2. Enable encryption - Use DoH if possible, DoT if not
3. Add Bloqr filters - Add your compiled Bloqr filter list to your resolver's configuration
4. Verify it works - Visit [1.1.1.1/help](https://1.1.1.1/help) to check connection status
5. Monitor your stats - Track queries and blocked domains in your resolver's dashboard
