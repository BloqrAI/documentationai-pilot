---
title: Can't Create an Account?
description: Fixes for the most common sign-up problems.
---

Work through these in order. Most sign-up problems are one of them.

## I never got the verification email

- Check your spam and junk folders.
- Check that you typed your email address correctly.
- Search your inbox for the word "verify" or "Bloqr".
- Try signing up again with the same email. Only the newest link works.

## It says my account already exists

You may already have an account. Try these:

- Sign in with GitHub or Google if you used one of them before. If the email matches, the same account opens.
- Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to reset your password.

## The sign-up button does nothing, or I see an error

- Reload the page and try again.
- Try a private window, or turn off browser extensions for the page. Some blockers interfere with sign-in.
- Try another sign-in method, such as GitHub or Google.

## I did all of that and I am still stuck

Ask in the chat on this page and say what you tried. A person can pick it up.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Tell us what you see**

  Include the exact error message and the sign-in method you used. Never send your password.
</Callout>
