  **Closed beta**

  Bloqr is in closed beta. Not everything works yet, and we will tell you plainly when something does not.
</Callout>

## Sign up

1. Go to [app.bloqr.dev](https://app.bloqr.dev) and choose **Sign Up**.
2. Pick how you want to sign in: email and password, GitHub, or Google.
3. With email and password, enter your name, email and a password. Bloqr may send you a verification email. Open it and click the link to activate your account.
4. With GitHub or Google, approve the request. Your account is created and linked for you.

## What you get

New accounts start on the free tier. You can choose your filter lists and get your personal DNS address as soon as you are signed in.
