## Get set up in 3 simple steps

1. Create your account at [app.bloqr.dev](https://app.bloqr.dev).
2. Choose your filter lists (what you want to block).
3. Copy your personal DNS address or download your compiled list and add it to your device or router.

## What Bloqr helps block

- **Block Ads**: Stops ad networks so pages load cleaner and with fewer interruptions.
- **Block Trackers**: Reduces hidden tracking tools that follow your activity across websites.
- **Block Malware**: Helps prevent known harmful domains from loading on your network.
