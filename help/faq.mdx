## Will Bloqr slow down my internet?

No. In many cases browsing feels faster because ads and tracking scripts do not load.

## Does Bloqr see my browsing history?

Bloqr is privacy-first. It focuses on DNS-level protection and does not need full browsing history to block known unwanted domains.

## How often does my list update?

Your lists update automatically. You do not need to manually refresh them.

## What if a website breaks?

You can report the issue and temporarily allow that site from your dashboard so you stay in control.

## Can I use Bloqr on multiple devices?

Yes. You can use the same Bloqr setup on your phone, laptop, desktop, and home network.

## What's the difference between the plans?

Plans differ by limits and features. See current details at [app.bloqr.dev billing and pricing](https://app.bloqr.dev/billing).

## Can I choose what gets blocked?

Yes. You can choose categories and sources in your dashboard.

## Is Bloqr free?

Yes, Bloqr includes a free tier so you can get started without paying.
