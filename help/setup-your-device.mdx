---
title: Use Bloqr on Your Device
description: Simple setup steps for phones, computers, and routers.
---

Your Bloqr DNS address is available on your dashboard at [app.bloqr.dev](https://app.bloqr.dev).

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Before you change anything**

  Save the restore steps on a second device or send them to yourself. If anything goes wrong, they get you back online in about two minutes.
</Callout>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone / iPad">
    1. Open **Settings** and tap **Wi-Fi**.
    2. Tap the info button next to your network.
    3. Open **Configure DNS** and switch to **Manual**.
    4. Enter your Bloqr DNS address, save, and reconnect.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open **Settings** and go to **Network & Internet**.
    2. Find **Private DNS**.
    3. Choose **Private DNS provider hostname**.
    4. Enter your Bloqr DNS address and save.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Windows">
    1. Open **Settings** and go to **Network & Internet**.
    2. Select your active connection and open DNS settings.
    3. Choose manual DNS setup.
    4. Enter your Bloqr DNS address and save.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Mac">
    1. Open **System Settings** and go to **Network**.
    2. Select your connection and open DNS settings.
    3. Add your Bloqr DNS address.
    4. Save changes and reconnect if needed.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Router / DNS Admin Panel">
    1. Open your router or DNS admin panel.
    2. Find the DNS settings section.
    3. Set Bloqr as the upstream or custom DNS address.
    4. Save and restart DNS service if prompted.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
