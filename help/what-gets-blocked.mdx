title: What Gets Blocked
description: "See exactly what Bloqr blocks, from ads and trackers to risky sites, with a plain example of each so you know what is protecting you."

Bloqr blocks common online nuisances and risky domains so your browsing feels cleaner and safer.

| Category | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ads | Promotional content that appears on pages and in apps. | Banner ads, autoplay ad videos |
| Trackers | Hidden services that collect activity data. | Cross-site tracking pixels |
| Malware domains | Known unsafe destinations used for harmful activity. | Fake download pages |
| Telemetry | Background data collection from apps and services. | Silent usage reporting calls |

Bloqr updates these blocking lists automatically, so you usually do not need to do anything after setup.
